About NutriAI

Democratizing Science-Backed Nutrition

We combine clinical physiology, evidence-based dietary research, and cutting-edge artificial intelligence to make world-class nutrition coaching accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Nutrition is inherently personal. Yet for decades, people have been trapped between restrictive generic diet fads and prohibitively expensive personal dietitians. NutriAI was built to bridge this gap: offering intelligent, empathetic, real-time nutrition guidance tailored to every individual's biology, lifestyle, and culture.

Whether your goal is athletic peak performance, healthy fat loss, managing insulin sensitivity, or simply eating more wholesome whole foods, NutriAI empowers you with clarity and sustainable habits.

Evidence-Based Formulas

We employ clinical gold standards including the Mifflin-St Jeor metabolic equations, WHO nutrient guidelines, and empirical glycemic index data.

Ethical & Private AI

Your biometric data, dietary logs, and health goals are strictly encrypted. We never monetize or sell your personal health records to third parties.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • NutriAI Technologies Inc.

At NutriAI, your personal health, biometric measurements, and dietary intake information are treated with the highest degree of confidentiality and data security. This policy outlines how we collect, safeguard, and utilize your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by you during registration and onboarding, including your age, biological sex, height, weight, activity frequency, dietary preferences, food allergies, and logged meals.

2. How AI Processes Your Data

NutriAI utilizes advanced machine learning models exclusively to calculate individualized caloric targets, generate customized meal suggestions, and respond to dietary inquiries. Your private logs are never used to train generalized third-party public AI models.

3. Zero-Sale Commitment

NutriAI does not sell, license, or rent your personal health data to insurance agencies, pharmaceutical advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Your Rights and Data Portability

You maintain complete ownership of your data. You may download your entire nutritional tracking history as a structured JSON file at any time via your Account Settings, or request complete account deletion.
